**We're upgrading our match commentary, as requested by so many of you!**

Starting in version 0.1.30, new English commentary is launching as a test for selected users in a few English-speaking countries. If you can't try it yet, don't worry: more languages are coming to more players soon.

Thank you for your patience and support as we keep making your matches more exciting.
