**Football League x Manchester City: the champions join the game.**

Players can now compete in authentic City kits, use the club's official badge, and access new in-game items inspired by one of football's most successful modern teams.

Whether you're building a new squad or chasing trophies in your favourite modes, you can now bring Manchester City's fast, fluid style of play onto the FL pitch. The "Sky Blues" are officially in the game.

Jump into Football League 2026, try the latest changes, and share your feedback with us on our community channels.
