**Version 0.1.33 of Football League 2024 is here**, with several key improvements:

- **Updated database:** the latest player and team info.
- **Bug fixes:** including freezes during free kicks and commentary changes.
- **Improved privacy:** the app no longer requests microphone permission.
- **New animations (testing):** in some countries, smoother and more dynamic short passes, long passes and tackles. Noticed the change? Let us know what you think!

Plus a limited-time **Road to Champion: 1990 Germany** event. Update now and step up your game!
