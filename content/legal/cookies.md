Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. We use as few as possible, and we only use non-essential cookies if you agree.

<p><button class="btn btn-primary" type="button" data-cookie-settings>Change cookie settings</button></p>

## Essential (always on)

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fl_consent` | Remembers your cookie choice | 12 months |

Our online shop (provided by Spreadshop) also sets the cookies it needs for the basket and checkout to work.

## Analytics (only with your consent)

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Tells visits apart, anonymously | 2 years |
| `_ga_*` | Google Analytics | Keeps track of the current visit | 2 years |

## Marketing (only with your consent)

If you allow marketing cookies, Google may use cookies to measure our ad campaigns and show relevant ads. We don't currently run other advertising trackers.

## Changing your choice

Use the **Change cookie settings** button above, or **Cookie Settings** in the footer of every page, at any time. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the site (such as the shop) may then not work.
